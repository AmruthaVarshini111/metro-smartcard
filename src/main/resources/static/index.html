Fare rules
  • You need at least ₹20 to enter.
  • Fares run from ₹10 to ₹60 by distance. Peak hours on weekdays (8 to 10 am, 5 to 7 pm) cost full price; other times are 10% off.
  • You never pay more than ₹100 in fares per day.
  • Forgetting to tap out, or tapping out without tapping in, costs a ₹50 penalty.

      What is under the hood

      • Routes use Dijkstra's algorithm on a graph, with interchanges as explicit edges.
      • Every top-up and tap carries an idempotency key, so a retry never charges twice.
      • Concurrent taps on one card are serialised, so the daily cap and balance cannot race.
      • Money is stored as whole paise, and the balance is the sum of an append-only ledger.
